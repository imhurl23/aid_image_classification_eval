The recovered adapter deliberately uses test.py. It does not select preprocessing
based on performance on our test images. This is model-native preprocessing of

Split evidence:
- data_preparation.py enumerates files with os.listdir and calls random.shuffle
  without a fixed seed; it places 90% in data/train and 10% in data/test.
- NeuralNets/train.py subsequently partitions data/train 80/20 with random_state=0.
  That seed fixes only the inner split, not the initial train/test allocation.
- demo.ipynb makes a new 80/20 split of data/raw with random_state=0 and unsorted
  os.listdir input. Its stored 95.8% evaluation is not evidence of held-out performance:
  that fresh split is not tied to the training checkpoint's original partition.
